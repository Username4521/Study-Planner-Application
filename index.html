Help

What every part of the planner does

🏠 Dashboard

Shows the current day, days left, topics done and today's topics for each subject (from your schedule). Use the arrows to move between days and log your study hours at the bottom. The day moves forward automatically each calendar day.

📅 Schedule

The day-by-day plan built during setup, dividing each subject's topics across your chosen days. Ticks show finished topics; today is highlighted. Use "Rebuild plan" to redo it.

⏱ Study Timer

A focus timer (25-min study, 5/10-min breaks or custom). Finished sessions are listed below it.

Day Planner

Set hours per subject, drag rows to choose the order, add specific requests, then generate a timed plan for today: two topics per subject, time sized by subtopics, exam-question practice, breaks, and review of weak areas from your mocks.

📝 Mock Exams

Enter weekly mock scores and a review of struggles and strengths. The next day's plan adds review time for them and pushes subjects below the 90% target.

📌 Notes & Reminders

A to-do list for reminders and key information. Tick off, pin, or delete notes.

📚 Subject pages

The checklist of topics and subtopics for the subjects you chose. Tick subtopics one by one, or tick a topic header to complete it all at once. "Video" finds lessons.

📊 Time Log

Every day's logged study hours with totals per subject.

⚙ Settings

Change days and current day, default hours, email recipients, export data, reset progress, or restart setup.

✉ Daily email

Once a day when the planner opens, a progress summary is emailed to the addresses in Settings.
